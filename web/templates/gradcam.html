How Grad-CAM Works

Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) shows which parts of an image are most important for the model's prediction.

1

Forward Pass

The image passes through the neural network to get predictions

2

Gradient Calculation

Gradients are computed with respect to the final convolutional layer

3

Weighted Sum

Feature maps are weighted by their gradient importance

4

Visualization

The result is upsampled and overlaid on the original image
